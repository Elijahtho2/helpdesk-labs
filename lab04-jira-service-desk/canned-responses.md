# Canned responses

Reusable replies for the GovLab Service Desk (Jira Service Management).

## Password reset done
> Hi, I've reset your password. I'll give you the temporary password over the phone. You'll be asked to choose a new one the first time you sign in. Reply here if anything goes wrong.

## Account unlocked
> Hi, your account was locked after several incorrect password attempts. I've unlocked it, so you can sign in now. If it locks again, reply here and I'll find the source.

## Escalated to Tier 2
> Hi, I've passed this to our Tier 2 team, who will pick it up next. You don't need to do anything right now. I'll update this ticket as soon as there's news.

## Access request needs approval
> Hi, access to this data has to be approved by its data owner. I've asked them to review your request. If they approve, I'll add the access and let you know. Until then I can't make the change.

## Suspicious email
> Hi, thanks for checking. Please don't click any links or reply. IT never asks for your password by email. Our security team is reviewing it.

## Outage update
> We're aware sign-ins are failing and are working on it. Next update in 15 minutes.
